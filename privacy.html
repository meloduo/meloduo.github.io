Meloduo

Privacy

Updated 24 November 2026. Meloduo is an alpha.

Short version: your projects stay in your browser. Meloduo has no accounts, no server of its own, no cookies and no analytics. A project leaves your computer only when you send it somewhere: to your AI agent, to a file or to a friend.

What stays in your browser

  • Projects, imported sounds and sound folders: in the browser's IndexedDB storage for this site.
  • Settings such as the layout, volume and export options: in the browser's localStorage.

We cannot see or restore this data. Clearing the site data in your browser deletes it. To keep a project safe, use File › Save project file.

What goes to your AI agent

Meloduo has no built-in AI. When you connect an agent, the bridge (@meloduo/mcp) runs on your computer and passes the project between the tab and your agent client, such as Claude Code. Your client then sends what the agent reads (notes, settings, render measurements) to its AI provider. That provider's privacy policy applies, not ours. If you never connect an agent, nothing goes to any AI provider.

Web plugins

When you add a web plugin by URL, your browser downloads it from that address and runs it on this page. The plugin's author can see the request and controls what the plugin does. Add plugins only from people you trust.

The feedback form

The form on the home page sends your message through Web3Forms to our mailbox. It sends your text, the page address, your email if you give one, and your browser version only if you tick that box on a bug report. We use it only to read and answer you.

Hosting

The site and the app are served by GitHub Pages. GitHub may log your IP address for security, as its privacy statement describes. We do not get these logs.
